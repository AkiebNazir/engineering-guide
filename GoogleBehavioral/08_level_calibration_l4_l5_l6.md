# Level Calibration: L4 vs L5 vs L6

This guide explains how to calibrate your behavioral stories for the target level.

## Scope and Impact

- **L4 (Mid-Level):** Scope is typically within a single team or feature. Impact is measured by successful delivery of assigned tasks, code quality, and learning.
- **L5 (Senior):** Scope spans multiple teams or a major system. Impact is measured by defining ambiguous problems, leading others, and changing team processes.
- **L6 (Staff):** Scope spans multiple organizations or the entire company. Impact is measured by setting technical vision, driving strategic alignment, and multiplying the effectiveness of multiple teams.

## Reshaping Stories

### Example: "Handling an Incident"

- **L4:** I was on-call, identified the failing service, rolled back the deployment, and wrote a post-mortem. I added a new test to prevent it.
- **L5:** I led the incident response across three teams, mitigating the immediate issue. I identified a systemic flaw in our deployment pipeline, proposed a new canary process, and drove its adoption across my org.
- **L6:** I noticed a pattern of related incidents across the company. I wrote a strategy document for a new company-wide reliability standard, secured executive buy-in, and mentored tech leads across 5 departments to implement it.

### Example: "Technical Decision"

- **L4:** I evaluated two database options for my feature, compared their latency, and picked the best one based on our team's existing guidelines.
- **L5:** I made a controversial technical choice that deviated from standard practices because of unique scale requirements. I built prototypes, presented data to senior engineers, and established a new pattern that other teams later adopted.
- **L6:** I drove the deprecation of a legacy technology stack across the company. I negotiated timelines with 10 different product teams, managed the migration risk, and ensured business continuity.

## Language and Framing

- **L4:** Emphasize execution, learning, and collaboration. Use "I built," "I learned," "I collaborated."
- **L5:** Emphasize leadership, ambiguity, and systemic change. Use "I defined," "I influenced," "I drove."
- **L6:** Emphasize strategy, vision, and organizational impact. Use "I aligned," "I initiated," "I sponsored."
